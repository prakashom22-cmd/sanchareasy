Common questions

What is an IP address?

An IP address is a number that identifies your connection on the internet, like a postal address for your device or router. IPv4 looks like 117.195.164.175 and IPv6 is a longer hexadecimal address.

Why is my IP address different from the one on my PC or router?

Your PC has a private IP such as 192.168.1.10, which only works inside your home network. The address shown on this page is your public IP, assigned by your ISP and shared by every device through your router using NAT.

Why does my IP location show the wrong city?

IP location comes from the ISP's registration data, so it usually shows the ISP's hub or nearest exchange instead of your exact town. It is accurate to the region, rarely to the street.

How do I find my private IP address on Windows, Android and iPhone?

On Windows, open Command Prompt and run ipconfig. On Android, go to Settings, Wi-Fi, then tap your network. On iPhone, go to Settings, Wi-Fi, then tap the info icon beside your network.

Is it safe to show my IP address?

Websites you visit can already see it. Sharing it does not give anyone access to your device, but avoid posting it publicly together with other personal details.

What is CIDR notation and a subnet mask?

CIDR such as /24 states how many bits of an IPv4 address identify the network. /24 equals the subnet mask 255.255.255.0 and gives 254 usable host addresses.
