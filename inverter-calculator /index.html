      What this cannot know

      Real appliance startup surge (get it from the nameplate or maker), the exact discharge table of the battery brand you buy (ask the dealer for the Ah at your backup hours, not the headline Ah), wire losses, and your actual outage pattern. Treat the output as a specification to verify, not a purchase order. Prices change too fast to include, so compare brands on usable kWh, warranty terms and cycle life at your depth of discharge.
